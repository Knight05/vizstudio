FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Do I need a developer to set this up?
No. Setup happens inside Data Studio — an easy add from our gallery. Styling and theming are point-and-click in the chart’s Style panel.
What happens to my charts if I cancel?
Your charts keep working through the end of your subscription. After that, simply swap them for Data Studio's default charts — your data and queries stay right where they are.
Can I use my own colors and fonts?
Yes. You can set custom colors on every chart, and most charts pick up your report's theme colors automatically for fast setup.
How often do you add new charts?
We're always building. The library keeps growing with new charts and improvements, and every customer gets every new chart — no tiers.
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes — full library, no credit card. Request access and we'll set you up by email. After the trial, pick Monthly or Annual.
Is my data sent to vizstudio servers?
No. Rendering happens inside Data Studio's sandbox — no data from your reports is ever accessed or collected by us.
