Pareto Chart

Pareto Chart for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Bars + cumulative line, the 80/20 rule, made obvious.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Pareto Chart sorts categories descending by value and overlays a cumulative percentage line: exactly the chart Joseph Juran intended for prioritization. The 80% cutoff is rendered automatically.

What it does

Descending sorted bars with a cumulative % line, optional 80% reference line, tooltip, and cross-filter.

Why use it

Prioritization conversations end here. The handful of bars before the cumulative line crosses 80% is your roadmap.

Three ways teams use it

QA
Defect cause analysis: top causes covering 80% of defects.
Ops
Cost driver Pareto for procurement.
Support
Top ticket categories.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
