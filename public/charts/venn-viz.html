What it is

Venn / Euler Diagram renders 2–4 set overlaps with circle sizes proportional to set size and intersection geometry placed correctly. For audience overlaps, feature overlaps, and any "who’s in all three?" question.

What it does

2–4 circle Venn/Euler diagram with sized circles, correct overlap geometry, count labels, tooltip.

Why use it

The Venn diagram every dashboard tries to fake with text. This one does the math.

Three ways teams use it

Marketing
Audience overlap across three campaigns.
Product
Users of features A, B, C.
Sales
Accounts in CRM × Newsletter × Webinar.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
