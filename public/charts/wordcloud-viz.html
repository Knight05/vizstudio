What it is

Word Cloud renders terms sized by frequency, optionally colored by category, with click-to-cross-filter. The fastest way to summarize unstructured text — and the chart your CMO actually asks for.

What it does

Frequency-weighted word layout with optional color-by-category and cross-filter on click.

Why use it

Sometimes the question is "what are people talking about?" — and the word cloud is the right answer, no apology.

Three ways teams use it

Support
Top ticket terms across a quarter.
Survey
Open-ended response themes.
Social
Top brand mentions in the news.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
