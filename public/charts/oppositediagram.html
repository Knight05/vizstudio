Opposite Diagram

Opposite Diagram for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Two-sided pros vs cons: opposing forces, one chart.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Opposite Diagram visualizes two opposing forces (pros vs cons, supports vs blocks, drivers vs detractors) as parallel rows of weighted bars. The strategic two-sider, ready for a slide.

What it does

Two parallel columns of bars, weighted, with center labels and per-side coloring.

Why use it

Decision frameworks live in tables; this chart turns them into a chart your audience can read.

Three ways teams use it

Strategy
Pros vs cons for a major decision.
UX
Usability strengths vs blockers.
M&A
Synergy vs risk for a target.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
