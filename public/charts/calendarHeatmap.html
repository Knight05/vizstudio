Mini Calendar

Mini Calendar for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Compact monthly calendar — one cell, one number, instant pattern.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Mini Calendar gives you a tight monthly grid with cells tinted by your metric — perfect for a small slot on a dashboard where a full calendar would dominate. Click a cell to cross-filter the rest of the page.

What it does

Compact monthly calendar with metric-tinted cells, tooltip on hover, cross-filter on click, and optional event highlighting.

Why use it

Day-of-week effects are visible without a full year heatmap.

Three ways teams use it

Marketing
This-month conversions tinted per day.
Ops
Incident counts for the current month.
Content
Posts published per day with engagement tint.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
