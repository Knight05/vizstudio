Bubble Map

Bubble Map for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Packed bubbles, sized by a metric — every category, no axis tax.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Bubble Map packs every category into a tight cluster of circles sized by your metric. With packed, force-directed, and grid layouts, plus gradient/single/series colors, it’s the catch-all for "show me everything, biggest to smallest."

What it does

Three layouts (packed, force, grid), three color modes (single, gradient, series), with inside/outside labels, tooltips, and click-to-cross-filter.

Why use it

Bar charts run out of room past 30 categories. Bubble map keeps reading well at 200.

Three ways teams use it

Marketing
All campaigns by spend, biggest in the center.
Ops
SKUs by units sold across the catalog.
HR
Headcount by team across the org.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
