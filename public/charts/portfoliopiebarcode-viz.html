Portfolio Pie Barcode

Portfolio Pie Barcode for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Portfolio pie + barcode — composition and per-position detail in one chart.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Portfolio Pie Barcode pairs the macro view (sector or asset-class pie) with the micro view (per-position barcode) in a single composition. The portfolio overview that doesn’t force a click-through.

What it does

Pie chart for top-level composition next to a barcode strip for every individual position; click a slice to filter the strip.

Why use it

Two questions — sector mix and per-position weight — answered on one canvas.

Three ways teams use it

Asset mgmt
Sector pie + position barcode for a daily review.
VC
Stage pie + investment barcode.
Pension fund
Asset class pie + holding barcode.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
