Half-pie: composition in a single horizontal slot.

Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Semi-Circle Pie Chart is the donut’s skinny cousin: perfect for headers, narrow dashboard slots, and any place a full pie eats too much vertical space.

What it does

Half-circle pie with category slices, optional center label, hover tooltip, cross-filter.

Why use it

Composition charts shouldn’t require half the dashboard. This is one row, full story.

Three ways teams use it

Dashboard headers
Quick category split above a KPI grid.
Mobile
Compact mix view that fits one column.
Marketing
Channel mix in a dashboard banner.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
