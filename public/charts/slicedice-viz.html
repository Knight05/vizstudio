What it is

Slice / Dice Treemap gives you three classic tiling algorithms — slice (horizontal stripes), dice (vertical stripes), or slice-dice (alternating) — so the structure of your hierarchy *looks* like hierarchy, not a random rectangle pile.

What it does

Treemap with slice/dice/slice-dice tiling, multi-dimension drilldown, click-to-zoom, breadcrumb navigation, theme-aware colors.

Why use it

Squarified treemaps prioritize compactness over structure. Slice/dice prioritizes the hierarchy itself — easier to read at depth.

Three ways teams use it

Finance
Sector → industry → ticker treemap.
Org
Division → team → person headcount.
Content
Category → tag → article volume.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
