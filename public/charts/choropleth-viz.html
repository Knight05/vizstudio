Choropleth Map for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Tile-grid map: every state or country, no projection pain.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Choropleth Map ditches the wonky shapefile rendering and lays out US states or world countries as a tile grid. Sequential, diverging, or binned color scales; square, rounded, hex, or circular tiles. Click a tile to cross-filter the page.

What it does

Accepts state abbreviations/names or ISO-2/ISO-3 country codes. Sequential/diverging/binned color, tile shape options, legend positioning, tooltip metric, cross-filter on click.

Why use it

Real-world map projections under-represent small dense states (hello, RI) and over-emphasize large empty ones. Tile grids give every entity equal voice.

Three ways teams use it

Sales
Revenue by state with the top 5 tiles called out.
Marketing
CPL by country across a global campaign.
Public sector
Indicator by region without distorting Alaska.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
