Revenue Breakdown (Bar + Line Combo)

Revenue Breakdown (Bar + Line Combo) for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Revenue mix bars + margin line — the public-company readout view.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Revenue Breakdown is a grouped bar chart with up to 4 bar metrics and an optional line overlay (e.g. Net Margin %). Dark theme with a green color scheme, legend, tooltip, cross-filter, and a Quarterly / Annual view toggle.

What it does

Grouped bars for up to 4 revenue components per period plus an optional overlay line for a ratio metric (margin %, etc.); Q/A view toggle.

Why use it

Designed for the way public-company earnings actually look — revenue mix + margin trend on one panel.

Three ways teams use it

Public co
Quarterly segment revenue + net margin %.
SaaS
New / expansion / churn / contraction bars + NRR line.
Retail
Channel mix bars + same-store growth line.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
