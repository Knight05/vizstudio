Grouped Bar Chart

Grouped Bar Chart for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Side-by-side bars per category — the comparison chart everyone reads correctly.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Grouped Bar Chart places two to four bars side-by-side within each category, sharing a y-axis and a legend. Cleaner than stacked when the absolute values per series matter more than the total.

What it does

Multi-series grouped bars per category with legend, tooltip, theme-aware colors, and cross-filter.

Why use it

Stacked bars compress the smaller series. Grouped bars give every series equal real estate.

Three ways teams use it

Sales
Current vs prior vs plan per region.
Marketing
Spend vs revenue vs leads per channel.
Operations
Volume by shift across multiple sites.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
