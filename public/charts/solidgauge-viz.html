Solid Gauge

Solid Gauge for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

A radial gauge for any "X out of target" story — without speedometer kitsch.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Solid Gauge fills a circular arc proportional to a metric’s progress toward a configurable target, with optional zones (red/amber/green) baked into the ring. Goal attainment, communicated cleanly.

What it does

270° or full-circle filled gauge with center value, %-to-target, and optional threshold bands.

Why use it

Goal pages need a single hero number — this gives them one with built-in context.

Three ways teams use it

Sales
Quota attainment — 78% of $4M.
Support
% tickets resolved within SLA.
Fundraising
% of campaign goal.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
