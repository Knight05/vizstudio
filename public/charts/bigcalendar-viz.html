Big Calendar

Big Calendar for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Full calendar grid — event volume painted across every cell.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Big Calendar lays out a month or year as a heat-tinted grid, with each day colored by your metric. It’s the dashboard equivalent of leaving a wall calendar open — instantly readable, no axis required.

What it does

Renders a full calendar grid (month or year), tints each day cell by a metric, supports tooltips, and cross-filters to the rest of the page.

Why use it

Time-of-week and time-of-year patterns jump out — the Tuesday slump, the Black Friday spike, the December lull.

Three ways teams use it

Ecommerce
Daily revenue colored across the year, holidays obvious.
Ops
Incident count per day in a year-at-a-glance heat layout.
HR
PTO calendar with team coverage at a glance.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
