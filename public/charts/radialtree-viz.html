Radial Tree

Radial Tree for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Radial hierarchy — branches arrayed around a center, every level visible.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Radial Tree lays out hierarchical data as branches radiating from a center, with node depth = ring and node size = metric. The most space-efficient way to show 3–5 hierarchy levels at once.

What it does

Hierarchical tree laid out radially with sized nodes, link curves, configurable depth coloring, hover-highlight.

Why use it

Trees grow tall fast. Radial layout keeps width and depth balanced.

Three ways teams use it

Org
Org tree from CEO outward.
Taxonomy
Product taxonomy four levels deep.
Content
Article category hierarchy.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
