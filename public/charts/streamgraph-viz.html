Streamgraph

Streamgraph for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Stacked area, no fixed baseline: flowing composition over time.

Add to Data Studio →
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Streamgraph stacks series around a central baseline that floats, producing flowing shapes that emphasize change in composition rather than totals. The chart for music genre share, ad spend mix, and any narrative "how did the wave shift?"

What it does

Floating-baseline stacked area with smooth interpolation, per-category color, tooltip, and label leaders.

Why use it

Best for showing how composition shifts when the absolute total isn’t the headline.

Three ways teams use it

Marketing
Channel share over years.
Media
Topic share in a news corpus.
Music
Genre share by decade.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
