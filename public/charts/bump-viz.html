Bump Chart

Bump Chart for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Rank-over-time line chart — who's climbing, who's sliding.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Bump Chart connects each category’s rank at every time step with a colored line. The eye follows the line — overtakes, slides, and surprises all read instantly. The animated cousin of a bar race, but every period on screen at once.

What it does

Plots category rank over time as smooth lines, with consistent per-category color, tooltips, and optional value labels at endpoints.

Why use it

Lines that cross are inherently storyable. Better than a stacked area when the categorical order matters.

Three ways teams use it

Marketing
Channel rank by attributed revenue across 12 months.
Sports
Conference standings week by week.
Product
Top 8 features by daily users over a quarter.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
