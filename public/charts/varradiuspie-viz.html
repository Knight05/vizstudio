Variable Radius Pie Chart

Variable Radius Pie Chart for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Variable-radius pie: angle is share, radius is a second metric.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Variable Radius Pie Chart (a.k.a. nightingale rose) keeps the angle = share rule but extends each slice’s radius by a second metric, so volume × magnitude reads at a glance.

What it does

Pie with each slice extended to a per-slice radius driven by a second metric, with tooltip and legend.

Why use it

Two metrics in one chart, in the form your audience already knows.

Three ways teams use it

Sales
Customer share by region (angle) × LTV (radius).
Marketing
Channel mix (angle) × conversion rate (radius).
Logistics
SKU share (angle) × margin (radius).

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
