Quadrant Scatter

Quadrant Scatter for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Four-quadrant scatter — the strategy chart you've drawn on whiteboards.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Quadrant Scatter splits your x/y plane into four labeled quadrants by configurable thresholds. Bubbles or dots, hover detail, cross-filter — the BCG / 2×2 framework that actually lives in your dashboard.

What it does

x/y scatter with vertical/horizontal threshold lines splitting the canvas into four labeled quadrants, optional bubble sizing.

Why use it

Strategy conversations are quadrant-shaped. Bring them into the dashboard.

Three ways teams use it

Strategy
Markets by growth × share, four quadrants labeled.
Product
Features by value × effort.
Sales
Accounts by potential × engagement.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
