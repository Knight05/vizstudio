Bullet Chart

Bullet Chart for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Stephen Few's gauge replacement — actual, target, and ranges in one bar.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Bullet Chart packs four data points — actual, target, qualitative ranges, and an optional comparative measure — into a compact horizontal bar. Designed to replace fluffy speedometers with dense, scannable performance bars.

What it does

Filled actual bar over banded ranges (poor / satisfactory / good), with a perpendicular tick for target and an optional comparative marker.

Why use it

More information per pixel than any gauge. Once teams adopt it, they don’t go back.

Three ways teams use it

Exec
Quarterly KPI scorecard — one bullet per KPI with target and good/poor bands.
Sales
Quota attainment vs. last year vs. plan.
Ops
SLA performance — actual against target band.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
