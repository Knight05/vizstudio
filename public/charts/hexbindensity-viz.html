Hexbin Density Plot

Hexbin Density Plot for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Hexagonal density plot: same hex grid, focused on point density.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Hexbin Density Plot is the density-only cousin of Hexbin Scatter: pure point-density coloring, ideal when you don’t need an avg-X or avg-Y mode and just want the where-do-records-cluster picture.

What it does

Hex grid with density coloring across two metrics, configurable bin size and color scale.

Why use it

When density alone is the answer, this is the leaner chart.

Three ways teams use it

Analytics
Where users cluster in a feature × tenure plane.
Quant
Trade size × hold-time density.
Geo
2D event density when projection isn’t needed.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
