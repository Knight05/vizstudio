Bubble Chart

Bubble Chart for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

X × Y × size: three metrics, one chart, zero squinting.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Bubble Chart encodes a third metric as circle size on top of a standard scatter, with optional category color. It’s the workhorse for any "big and growing" / "small and shrinking" story.

What it does

Renders circles sized by a third metric on x/y axes, with optional category coloring, hover tooltip, and click-to-filter.

Why use it

Adds a magnitude dimension without forcing a second chart. The eye reads area and position simultaneously.

Three ways teams use it

Strategy
Markets by revenue × growth × size (the BCG-matrix you actually deploy).
Sales
Accounts by deal count × ACV × pipeline.
Product
Features by usage × satisfaction × engineering cost.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
