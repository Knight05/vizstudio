Portfolio Barcode

Portfolio Barcode for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Portfolio barcode: every holding as a vertical strip, weighted and colored.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Portfolio Barcode renders each holding as a narrow vertical strip, width or color encoding weight and category. Dense, ordered, and surprisingly readable for portfolios of 100+ positions.

What it does

Vertical barcode strips per holding, with width-or-color encoding for weight or category, hover detail, cross-filter on click.

Why use it

Traditional portfolio pies fail past 20 holdings. Barcode keeps every position visible.

Three ways teams use it

Asset mgmt
Equity book at sector glance.
Crypto
Token positions by market cap.
VC
Fund holdings by stage.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
