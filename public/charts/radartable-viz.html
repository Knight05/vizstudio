What it is

Radar Chart with Data Table pairs a polygon radar across N metrics with a sortable table of the underlying values. The radar tells the shape story; the table answers "what was the exact number?" without a second view.

What it does

Radar polygon per category over shared metric axes, alongside a sortable data table with the values, theme-aware coloring.

Why use it

Radar charts are good at shape, bad at precision. Adding the table fixes the only complaint about radars.

Three ways teams use it

Product
Feature comparison: us vs competitor across attributes.
HR
Team capability radar across competencies.
Sales
Account fit score across criteria.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
