Hexbin Scatter

Hexbin Scatter for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Hexagonal density: scatter plots that survive 100k points.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Hexbin Scatter bins two numeric metrics onto a pointy-top hexagon grid and shades each cell by density, average X, or average Y. Optional Category dimension surfaces top-3 categories in tooltips and joins cross-filters. 12 palettes, 4 density scales, optional trend line and marginals.

What it does

Hexagon bin grid over x/y metrics; density / avg-X / avg-Y color modes; 12 palettes, 4 scales (linear/sqrt/log/power); optional trend line; optional marginal distributions; min-count filter; click cross-filter.

Why use it

Scatter overplots at scale. Hexbin keeps every region readable and surfaces hot zones immediately.

Three ways teams use it

Marketing
CTR × CPC density across thousands of keywords.
Product
Session length × pages-viewed density.
Finance
Risk × return density across positions.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
