What it is

Stacked Bar Chart with Negative Values handles the case standard stacked bars fail: a mix of positive and negative contributions stacked separately above and below the zero line, with the net total visible.

What it does

Positive series stacked above zero, negative stacked below, with optional net-total line per category and tooltip.

Why use it

Most charting tools force negatives into separate panes. This keeps them on the same chart, the way the data is structured.

Three ways teams use it

Finance
Revenue contributions vs returns per period.
Climate
Emissions vs offsets per year.
Sales
New bookings vs churn per quarter.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel — done.
