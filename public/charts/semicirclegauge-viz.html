Semicircle Gauge

Semicircle Gauge for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Half-circle gauge: same story, half the real estate.

Add to Data Studio →
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Semicircle Gauge delivers the same goal-attainment narrative as a full circle in a slim half-arc footprint. Pair with a tile-style layout to fit six KPIs in a single row.

What it does

180° arc filled to current value with threshold bands and a center metric.

Why use it

Looks deliberate next to KPI cards; fits where a full circle won’t.

Three ways teams use it

Mobile dashboards
Tile-style KPI rows.
Risk register
Likelihood score per risk.
Health
% of daily goal in a slim card.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
