Chart Title

Chart Title for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Two metrics, two scales, one line of sight: without the lie factor.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

Dual Axis pairs two metrics on independent y-axes: bars for volume, line for rate, or any combination. Carefully designed scales and ticks keep the chart honest while staying readable.

What it does

Bar + line, or two lines, with independent scaled y-axes, shared x-axis, legend, tooltip, and cross-filter.

Why use it

Volume and rate often need the same chart and never the same scale. Dual axis solves that without forcing a second visualization.

Three ways teams use it

Marketing
Spend (bars) and CPL (line) over time.
Product
Sessions (bars) and conversion % (line).
Sales
Pipeline volume (bars) and win rate % (line).

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
