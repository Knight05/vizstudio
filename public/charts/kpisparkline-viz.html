KPI Sparkline Cards

KPI Sparkline Cards for Data Studio, formerly known as Looker Studio.

Headline numbers with trend lines baked in, the executive scorecard, refined.

Add to Data Studio → Open live demo
Cross-filter ready Theme-aware Setup in 30s

What it is

KPI Sparkline Cards combine a large headline metric, a delta vs. prior period, and an inline sparkline so leaders can see the number, the change, and the trajectory in a single glance.

What it does

Grid of KPI cards, each with primary metric, %-change indicator, and a 30/60/90-day sparkline.

Why use it

Replaces three separate scorecards with one focused row. The sparkline kills the "but is that good?" follow-up.

Three ways teams use it

Exec
MBR: Revenue, MRR, Churn %, NPS across the top.
SaaS
DAU, WAU, activation, retention with mini-trends.
Marketing
Spend, Leads, CPL, Conversion at a glance.

Add it in 30 seconds

  1. Open your Data Studio report and click Add a chart → Community visualizations → Explore more.
  2. Paste the Viz Studio manifest URL or pick this chart from the Viz Studio gallery card.
  3. Bind the dimensions and metrics in the data panel. Done.
